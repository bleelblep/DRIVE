About This Archive

This archive serves as a digital preservation project dedicated to celebrating and documenting the groundbreaking work of SOPHIE—a visionary artist, producer, and DJ who redefined electronic music and left an indelible mark on contemporary pop culture.

From the hyperreal textures of early singles like "BIPP" and "Lemonade" to the bold sonic experimentation of the album OIL OF EVERY PEARL'S UN-INSIDES, SOPHIE's work transcended genre boundaries and challenged conventional notions of sound, identity, and artistry.

This collection aims to make these materials accessible to fans, researchers, and anyone interested in exploring the legacy of one of the 21st century's most innovative artists. Each section is carefully curated to provide comprehensive access to various aspects of SOPHIE's creative output and cultural impact.
