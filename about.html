About This Archive

This archive serves as a digital preservation project dedicated to celebrating and documenting the groundbreaking work of SOPHIE—a visionary artist, producer, and DJ who redefined electronic music and left an indelible mark on contemporary pop culture.

From the hyperreal textures of early singles like "BIPP" and "Lemonade" to the bold sonic experimentation of the album OIL OF EVERY PEARL'S UN-INSIDES, SOPHIE's work transcended genre boundaries and challenged conventional notions of sound, identity, and artistry.

This collection aims to make these materials accessible to fans, researchers, and anyone interested in exploring the legacy of one of the 21st century's most innovative artists. Each section is carefully curated to provide comprehensive access to various aspects of SOPHIE's creative output and cultural impact.

SOPHIE (1986-2021)

SOPHIE was a Scottish musician, producer, DJ, and trans icon who pushed the boundaries of pop music and electronic production. Known for creating immersive, otherworldly soundscapes that felt both hyperreal and deeply emotional, SOPHIE collaborated with artists like Charli XCX, Vince Staples, Madonna, and countless others, shaping the sound of modern pop music.

Her 2018 debut album OIL OF EVERY PEARL'S UN-INSIDES was nominated for a Grammy Award for Best Dance/Electronic Album and received widespread critical acclaim for its innovative production, genre-defying sound, and deeply personal themes.

SOPHIE's influence extends far beyond music—she was a pioneer in trans visibility in electronic music and used her platform to advocate for authenticity, transformation, and creative freedom. Her impact on music production, pop culture, and the LGBTQ+ community continues to resonate.

Our Mission

The Drive exists to preserve SOPHIE's artistic legacy for future generations. We believe that art of this caliber should be accessible, discoverable, and protected from the ephemeral nature of digital media.

This is a community-maintained, unofficial archive. We respect all copyright holders and encourage everyone to support official releases, streams, and purchases whenever possible.

If you have materials to contribute or suggestions for the archive, please reach out to the community. Together, we keep SOPHIE's vision alive.
